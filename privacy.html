Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Zero Account & Client-Side Privacy

Mockhard is built on a Zero Account Architecture. You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal information (such as email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords) to use the platform.

All test progress, scores, bookmarks, accuracy metrics, and custom test history are stored exclusively in your device's browser local storage (localStorage) and session storage.

2. What Data Is Collected

  • Test History: Scores, completion dates, time taken, and accuracy breakdowns per category.
  • Question Bookmarks: Saved questions bookmarked for review.
  • User Preferences: Dark mode / Light mode theme setting.

3. Data Ownership & Export / Deletion

Your data belongs entirely to you. You can export a backup of your progress in JSON format at any time from the Dashboard or clear your data by clearing your browser cache.

4. Analytics & Third Parties

Mockhard does not use invasive tracking cookies or sell user data to third-party advertisers. All question banks are served static via GitHub Pages.
